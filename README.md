# The Six Second Pitch Toolkit

**The fast route to a message you own, a page you can use, and leads who understand why you matter.**
Five free Claude skills that work as a team. By [Dee Patience](https://sixsecondpitch.com).
Free to use. Always the latest version.

---

## What this is

Most people ask AI to "write me a pitch" and get generic words that sound like everyone else.
This toolkit works the way a real marketing team works: it learns YOUR brand once, researches
your buyer's real words before writing anything, scores your message against a rubric, and
refuses to ship work that fails its own quality checks.

## Get the toolkit

No terminal. No GitHub account. Nothing to install on your computer. If you can drag a file, you can do this.

**Before you start: a 60-second check.**
1. Know your plan. In Claude, click your initials, then Settings, then Billing. Any paid plan (Pro and up) is what we test on.
2. Turn on Code execution: Settings, then Capabilities. The tools need it to build your files.
3. Claude updates often, so menus can move. The official guide stays current: [Use skills in Claude](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12512180-use-skills-in-claude). Bookmark it.

**Install:**
1. Download the 5 tools from the [`zips/` folder](zips) in this repo (they are also on [sixsecondpitch.com/toolkit](https://sixsecondpitch.com/toolkit)). They are small zip files. Do not unzip them.
2. In Claude, go to Customize, then Skills. Click Add, then Upload a skill. Drag 1 zip in.
3. Repeat for the other 4. Claude takes them 1 at a time.
4. Start a new chat and say: **"Set up my toolkit."**

That last step starts a 10-minute interview about your brand. It saves your answers so you never repeat yourself.

## Meet the team

Use them in this order the first time. After that, The Navigator routes you.

### 1. The Kickoff (`ssp-toolkit-onboarding`): run this FIRST, once
A friendly interview about your brand and how you work. It writes your "Team Bible":
four files the other skills read at the start of every session, so you never repeat yourself.
**Say:** "Set up my toolkit."

### 2. The Navigator (`ssp-toolkit-navigator`): your project manager
Reads your Team Bible, confirms what you are trying to do, and sends the work to the right
tool. It also runs a quality check before anything is shown or shipped.
**Say:** "Help me with my business" or just describe what you need.

### 3. The Six Second Pitch (`ssp-analyzer`): your message coach
Scores your pitch or website against the Person, Problem, Promise framework and gives you
a Clarity Score. Five modes, from a quick read of your homepage to a full council stress test.
**Say:** "Analyze my pitch" or "Score my website: [your URL]."

### 4. Audience Recon (`ama-research-engine`): your market researcher and writer
Pulls the real words your buyers use (from reviews, forums, and communities) BEFORE any
copy gets written. That is the rule that makes the writing sound like your customer, not like AI.
**Say:** "Research my audience" or "Run voice-of-customer research for [your product]."

### 5. Route Review (`skill-trainer`): your quality system
After a piece of work, it reviews what went well and what did not, asks before fixing, and
folds the lessons back into the tools so the same mistake does not happen twice.
**Say:** "Lessons learned" or "Review what we just did."

## The one rule that makes it work

**Research before writing.** The toolkit will not write your pitch, page, or email until
Audience Recon has pulled your buyer's real words. Skipping research is how you get
generic AI copy. The tools enforce the order so you do not have to remember it.

## Stuck? Read this first

**"Do I need a GitHub account?"** No. You only need your Claude login.

**"Which Claude plan do I need?"** Any paid Claude plan (Pro and up) works. That is what we test on. Claude lists skills on the Free plan too ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12512180-use-skills-in-claude)), but if you are on Free and hit a snag, email me.

**"It says the file must include SKILL.md."** You probably unzipped the file first. Upload the zip itself, not the folder inside it.

**"My upload stopped."** Upload 1 zip at a time. Dragging several at once can fail without saying why.

**"Is the problem Claude or the toolkit?"** If Claude will not take an upload, a menu is missing, or a setting will not turn on, that is Claude's side. Check [Use skills in Claude](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12512180-use-skills-in-claude) or [support.claude.com](https://support.claude.com). If a toolkit tool gives a wrong or confusing answer, that is mine.

Still stuck on the toolkit itself? Email [hello@deepatience.com](mailto:hello@deepatience.com) or visit [sixsecondpitch.com](https://sixsecondpitch.com). Dee reads everything.
